How this is calculated

The estimate is length × log₂(pool size), where the pool is every character your settings allow. It assumes each character is picked at random, which VAULT does. Requiring one character of each type reduces it very slightly, so treat it as an upper bound.

Weak: under 45 bits · Fair: 45–64 · Strong: 65–89 · Excellent: 90+

This rates your settings, not a promise of safety. Use a different password for every account and keep them in a password manager.

Passwords are generated when you ask, sent only to your browser, and never saved or logged.
